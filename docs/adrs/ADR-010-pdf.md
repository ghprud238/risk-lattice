# ADR-010: Document Architecture & Normative Precedence

## Status
Accepted

## Context
ContagionGraph architecture specification (Extended PRD v2.1 Section 29).

## Decision
Original pages 1-9 preserved unchanged; extended specifications appended in pages 10-71 with Section 9 precedence.

## Revisit Trigger
Refactoring PRD into modular multi-repo RFCs.

## Consequences
- Guarantees deterministic mathematical reproducibility and strict privacy separation.
- Prevents database deadlocks and accidental vendor lock-in.
