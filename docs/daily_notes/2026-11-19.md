## 1. Executive Summary

Today we expanded RiskLattice into a unified cross-platform institutional platform by architecting, implementing, and verifying a dedicated **React Native / Expo mobile application** (`mobile/`) designed specifically for Chief Risk Officers (CROs) and Portfolio Managers requiring real-time threat telemetry and emergency stress-testing outside the trading floor.

### Key Architectural Achievements:
1. **Strict Client-Server Division of Labor**:
   - **Symbolic Computational Engine**: Remains 100% on the FastAPI backend (`backend/app/engine/*.py`) under strict AST import isolation (ADR-002: no `duckdb`, `fastapi`, `httpx`, or `litellm` imports). Runs heavy 10,000-path Student-$t$ Copula Monte Carlo simulations, Huber loss calibrations, and portfolio balance-sheet revaluations.
   - **Web Client (`frontend/`)**: React 19 / Next.js 15 on port 5050 for complex multi-asset Cytoscape.js network canvas editing, 0-90 day dynamic timeline scrubbing, and raw CSV portfolio blotter imports.
   - **Mobile Client (`mobile/`)**: React Native & Expo SDK 58 (`react: 19.3.0`, `react-native: 0.88.1`) for real-time AIS maritime congestion radar, 1-tap shock injection, 3-stage PnL waterfall triage, conversational AI Copilot chat, and 1-tap Four-Eyes cryptographic dual sign-offs.
2. **Unified Type Safety (`shared/types/risk-lattice.ts`)**:
   - Single source of truth for TypeScript data contracts across Web and Mobile: `ThreatSnapshot`, `ChokePointTelemetry`, `SimulationResult`, `PortfolioPnL`, `AuditEntry`, `CopilotResponse`, and `UserProfile`.
3. **Institutional Mobile UX & Dark Theme (`mobile/src/theme/colors.ts`)**:
   - High-contrast institutional dark palette tailored for mobile OLED displays (`#080c14` background, `#141e33` surface, `#38bdf8` cyber sky, `#ef4444` shock red, `#10b981` hedge emerald, `#06b6d4` AIS cyan).
4. **Resilient Network Gateway & Mock Layer (`mobile/src/config/api.ts`)**:
   - Dynamic host switching with one-tap presets for iOS Simulator (`http://localhost:5051`), Android Emulator (`http://10.0.2.2:5051`), and physical devices over LAN Wi-Fi (`http://<LAN_IP>:5051`).
   - Integrated offline mock fallback providing mathematically grounded simulation and AIS data when running decoupled from the backend.

## 2. Mobile App Architecture & Screen Breakdown

The mobile application is structured into five dedicated operational screens coordinated via an institutional tab bar:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                               RISKLATTICE MOBILE (Expo)                         │
├─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────┬─────────────┤
│  1. RADAR       │  2. STRESS      │  3. COPILOT     │  4. AUDIT   │  5. CONFIG  │
│  AIS Telemetry  │  Factor Shocks  │  Conversational │  Four-Eyes  │  Gateway &  │
│  & SVI Index    │  & PnL Cascade  │  CRO Assistant  │  Dual-Seal  │  Personas   │
└─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────┴─────────────┘
```

### Detailed Screen Capabilities:
1. **Radar Screen (`RadarScreen.tsx`)**:
   - **Systemic Vulnerability Index (SVI $0-100$)**: Circular animated gauge displaying aggregate maritime vulnerability.
   - **5 Choke Points Monitored**: Taiwan Strait, Malacca Strait, Suez Canal, Strait of Hormuz, and Panama Canal with active vessel queue counts, speed collapse %, and demurrage surge.
   - **Automated Polling**: Configurable 8-second refresh loop with manual "+1 Hr Drift Tick" advance button.
   - **Live Threat Wire**: Breaking geopolitical alerts with a **1-Tap "⚡ Inject Shock"** button that immediately loads the scenario into the stress engine.
2. **Stress Screen (`StressScreen.tsx`)**:
   - **Institutional Factor Grid**: 1-tap selection of `FOUNDRY_TW`, `BALTIC_DRY`, `BRENT_CRUDE`, and `SOFR_SPREAD`.
   - **Interactive Shock Sliders**: Dynamic adjustment from $-15\%$ to $-60\%$.
   - **3-Stage PnL Waterfall Card**: Visual proportional breakdown across Direct 1st Order, Supplier 2nd Order, and Liquidity Contagion 3rd/4th Order.
   - **Institutional Risk Cards**: 99% Student-$t$ Copula CVaR, Tail Ratio ($12.2\times$), and top Marginal Contribution to Risk (MCR) contributors.
3. **Copilot Screen (`CopilotScreen.tsx`)**:
   - **Conversational CRO Assistant**: Natural language risk triage grounded in exact quantitative models.
   - **Fast Prompt Chips**: Instant queries for supply-chain vulnerability, hedge recommendations, and foundry shock exposures.
   - **Verbatim SEC 10-K Citations**: Responses feature exact character-offset quotes and citations to ensure zero hallucination.
4. **Audit Screen (`AuditScreen.tsx`)**:
   - **Four-Eyes Governance (SR 11-7)**: Lists historical stress simulations pending executive sign-off.
   - **1-Tap Biometric Dual-Sign & Seal**: Secondary approval action that cryptographically signs the run, generates an immutable SHA-256 seal, and updates audit records.
5. **Settings Screen (`SettingsScreen.tsx`)**:
   - **Gateway Host Switcher**: Quick-connect buttons for Localhost, Android Emulator, and LAN IP.
   - **Live Server Ping**: Round-trip health check utility displaying latency in milliseconds.
   - **4-Tier Persona Switcher**: Switch active identity between CRO, Portfolio Manager, Risk Analyst, and Internal Auditor.

## 3. Institutional Beta Testing Strategy

To validate RiskLattice prior to commercial monetization, we formulated an institutional beta partner framework targeting four high-value cohorts:

| Cohort | Target Institutions | Primary Pain Point Solved | Key Decision Maker |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Multi-Strategy Hedge Funds** | Citadel, Millennium, Balyasny, Point72 | Hidden 2nd/3rd order supply-chain factor risks uncaptured by MSCI Barra / Axioma | Chief Risk Officer / Quantitative PM |
| **G-SIBs & Investment Banks** | JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs | Fed CCAR FR Y-14A Schedule A compliance & SR 11-7 model governance | Head of Model Risk / Head of Stress Testing |
| **Corporate Supply Chain Treasuries** | Apple, Ford, Boeing, Nvidia | Days-of-inventory exhaustion & demurrage surge forecasting | Group Treasurer / VP Supply Chain Risk |
| **Reinsurance & Specialty Underwriters** | Swiss Re, Munich Re, Lloyd's syndicates | Supply-chain contingent business interruption (CBI) tail risk | Chief Underwriting Officer |

### 4-Week Design Partner Pilot Framework:
- **Week 1**: Ingestion of masked portfolio holdings (Rule P-01 privacy verified).
- **Week 2**: Custom choke-point calibration & historical crisis replay backtesting.
- **Week 3**: Executive mobile app onboarding for traveling CROs & PMs.
- **Week 4**: Fed SR 11-7 validation dossier delivery & board memo review.
