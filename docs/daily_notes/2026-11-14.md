## 1. Executive Summary

Today we completed the institutional enterprise transformation of RiskLattice, delivering end-to-end backend engines, API gateways, interactive frontend modules, and strict governance controls for tier-1 hedge funds and sovereign asset allocators:

1. **Google Workspace Enterprise SSO & RBAC Authentication Gateway**:
   - Built [`backend/app/services/auth_service.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/risk-lattice/backend/app/services/auth_service.py) with Google OAuth ID Token (JWT) verification, HMAC-SHA256 session token management, and institutional RBAC roles (`CHIEF_RISK_OFFICER`, `PORTFOLIO_MANAGER`, `ANALYST`, `AUDITOR`).
   - Integrated full frontend auth gateway: [`frontend/src/context/AuthContext.tsx`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/risk-lattice/frontend/src/context/AuthContext.tsx) and [`frontend/src/components/LoginScreen.tsx`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/risk-lattice/frontend/src/components/LoginScreen.tsx) with Citadel, Point72, and JPMorgan institutional sandbox personas.
2. **Feature 1: Capital-Efficient Tail-Risk Hedge Optimizer (`backend/app/engine/optimizer.py`)**:
   - Implemented convex Sequential Least Squares Programming (SLSQP) solver:
     $$\max_{x} \sum_{j} x_j \cdot (\text{unit\_protection}_j - \text{cost}_j) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \sum_j x_j \cdot \text{cost}_j \le \text{Budget}, \quad 0 \le x_j \le \text{MaxNotional}_j$$
   - Solves for minimum-cost hedging baskets across equity put options, single-name CDS, and commodity futures.
   - Computes parametric efficient frontier curves mapping premium expenditure to $CVaR_{99}$ compression.
3. **Feature 2: Reverse Stress Testing & Minimal Breaking Point (`backend/app/engine/optimizer.py`)**:
   - Inverted the contagion propagation kernel to identify the most probable macro/supply failure vectors:
     $$\min_{F} \|F\|_2^2 \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \text{Portfolio\_Loss}(F) \ge \text{Target\_Drawdown\_Threshold}$$
   - Identifies critical vulnerability factors and computes $L_2$ distance norms of crisis scenarios.
4. **Feature 3: Dynamic Multi-Period Timeline Simulation (`backend/app/engine/timeline.py`)**:
   - Discrete multi-period horizon engine ($t \in [0, 7, 14, 30, 45, 60, 90\text{ days}]$) simulating wave-by-wave transmission delays ($t_{\text{arrival}} \approx d \times 7\text{d}$).
   - Dynamically tracks remaining safety stock inventory buffers ($\tau_{\text{rem}}(t) = \max(0, \tau_{\text{inv}} - t)$), solvency multiplier escalation upon buffer depletion, and generates chronological milestone alerts.
5. **Feature 5: One-Click Board-Ready Executive PDF/Memo Generator (`backend/app/services/report_generator_service.py`)**:
   - Produces institutional investment committee memoranda formatted in structured JSON and print-ready CSS `@media print` HTML.
   - Formulates executive drawdown gauges, loss waterfall attributions, critical path routes, verbatim SEC 10-K risk citations, and Federal Reserve SR 11-7 model governance sign-off declarations.
6. **Feature 6: Side-by-Side Scenario Diffing & A/B Risk Comparison (`backend/app/engine/diffing.py`)**:
   - Computes $\Delta S_v = S_v^{(B)} - S_v^{(A)}$, $\Delta \text{PnL}$, $\Delta \text{CVaR}_{99}$, and relative risk shifts.
   - Categorizes portfolio nodes into `AMPLIFIED` (loss worsened $>15\%$), `DE_RISKED` (loss reduced $>15\%$), and `UNCHANGED`.
7. **Feature 7: Four-Eyes Principle Sign-off & Audit Vault (`backend/app/services/audit_vault_service.py`)**:
   - Enforces institutional risk governance lifecycle:
     $$\text{DRAFT} \longrightarrow \text{PENDING\_REVIEW} \longrightarrow \text{APPROVED\_BY\_CRO} \longrightarrow \text{LOCKED\_REGULATORY}$$
   - Strictly enforces identity separation: the scenario submitter cannot approve their own run (Four-Eyes principle).
   - Generates 64-character SHA-256 tamper-evident cryptographic digital seals over portfolio, model parameters, and scenario results.
8. **Interactive UI Dashboards (`frontend/src/components/`)**:
   - `HedgeOptimizer.tsx`: Sliders for premium budget and target loss, allocation table, efficient frontier.
   - `TimelinePlayer.tsx`: Interactive scrubber across 0-90 days, inventory depletion bars, and milestone feed.
   - `ScenarioDiffViewer.tsx`: Component differential matrix with status filtering.
   - `ExecutiveMemoModal.tsx`: Board-ready printable memo with `window.print()` PDF generation.
   - `RegulatorySignoffModal.tsx`: Four-Eyes sign-off modal with CRO approval and regulatory locking.
