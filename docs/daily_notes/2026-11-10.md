### 2.3 Milestone 2: Mathematical Engine & Copula Simulation
- **Goal**: Deterministic, bit-exact financial risk and multi-hop contagion propagation engine.
- **Implementation**:
  - **Huber Robust Loss Edge Calibrator** ([`backend/app/engine/calibrator.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/contagion-graph/backend/app/engine/calibrator.py)):
    - Calibrates inter-firm dependency weights $W_{uv}$ using robust regression resistant to financial return outliers:
      $$L_\delta(r) = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{2} r^2 & \text{for } |r| \le \delta \\ \delta \left(|r| - \frac{1}{2}\delta\right) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
  - **4-Hop Damped Contagion Propagator** ([`backend/app/engine/propagator.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/contagion-graph/backend/app/engine/propagator.py)):
    - Recursive message-passing kernel:
      $$\Delta s_v^{(k)} = \alpha^{k-1} \sum_u W_{uv} \cdot \Delta s_u^{(k-1)} \cdot (1 - \sigma_v) \cdot \lambda_v$$
    - **Decay Factor**: $\alpha = 0.65$ per hop, ensuring energy conservation and convergence.
    - **Supplier Substitutability**: $\sigma_v \in [0.0, 0.8]$ dampening shocks when alternative suppliers exist.
    - **Non-Linear Distress Multiplier**:
      $$\lambda_v = \begin{cases} 1 + 0.35 \cdot \max(0, \text{NetDebt/EBITDA} - 3.0), & \text{if } \text{NetDebt/EBITDA} > 3.0 \text{ and } \Delta s < 0 \\ 1.0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
    - Shock bounds clamped strictly to $[-0.95, +1.50]$.
  - **Student-$t$ Copula Monte Carlo Tail Risk Engine** ([`backend/app/engine/risk_metrics.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/contagion-graph/backend/app/engine/risk_metrics.py)):
    - 10,000-path Monte Carlo joint tail simulation.
    - Heavy-tailed multivariate Student-$t$ distribution ($\nu = 5$ degrees of freedom) capturing joint crash probabilities that Gaussian copulas miss.
    - Computes 99% Parametric VaR, Historical VaR, Copula VaR, Expected Shortfall (CVaR 99%), and Marginal Contribution to Risk (MCR).
  - **Historical Crisis Replay Backtesting** ([`backend/app/engine/backtest.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/contagion-graph/backend/app/engine/backtest.py)):
    - Implemented 4 canonical crisis regimes:
      1. *2008 Lehman Liquidity Freeze* (`HY_OAS: +8.5%`, `SOFR_SPREAD: +3.5%`)
      2. *2020 COVID Mobility Halt* (`BRENT: -55%`, `BALTIC_DRY: -48%`)
      3. *2022 Rate & Duration Shock* (`US10Y_REAL: +2.6%`, `DXY: +14.0%`)
      4. *Synthetic Taiwan Foundry Halt* (`FOUNDRY_TW: -70%`, `BALTIC_DRY: +180%`)
    - Implemented Kupiec Proportion of Failures (POF) statistical test ($\chi^2_1$) to validate model calibration.
  - **Verification Gate**: Passed `backend/tests/test_math.py` with 100% bit-exact numerical assertions.

#### 3. Analytical Numerical Test Suite ($N\text{-}01$ to $N\text{-}12$)
- Created [`backend/tests/unit/test_analytical_cases.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/contagion-graph/backend/tests/unit/test_analytical_cases.py):
  - **N-01 (Single Node Identity)**: Direct shock to isolated node propagates zero shock to unlinked nodes.
  - **N-02 (Two Node Linear Transmission)**: Hand-calculated 1-hop transmission matches $W_{12} \cdot s_1^{(0)} \cdot (1 - \sigma_2)$.
  - **N-03 (Three Node Chain)**: 2-hop transmission equals $W_{12} \cdot W_{23} \cdot \alpha \cdot (1 - \sigma_2)(1 - \sigma_3)$.
  - **N-04 (Hop Damping Monotonicity)**: Shock energy monotonically decreases across successive hops: $||\Delta s^{(k+1)}|| < ||\Delta s^{(k)}||$.
  - **N-05 (Substitutability Nullification)**: When $\sigma_v = 1.0$, node $v$ absorbs zero incoming supply shock.
  - **N-06 (Distress Multiplier Threshold)**: Unlevered firm ($\text{Debt/EBITDA} = 1.0$) has $\lambda = 1.0$; levered firm ($6.0$) exhibits $\lambda = 2.05$.
  - **N-07 (Distress Sign Asymmetry)**: Positive shocks ($\Delta s > 0$) never trigger distress amplification ($\lambda = 1.0$).
  - **N-08 (Huber Robustness Outlier Resistance)**: Synthetic outlier in asset returns does not corrupt calibrated edge sensitivity.
  - **N-09 (Extreme Shock Clamping)**: Shocks exceeding bounds are strictly clamped to $[-0.95, +1.50]$.
  - **N-10 (Cyclic Feedback Stability)**: Directed 2-node cycle converges stably without infinite explosion due to $\alpha = 0.65$.
  - **N-11 (Portfolio Non-Linear Convexity)**: High-beta leveraged positions exhibit accelerated losses under compounding shocks.
  - **N-12 (Bit-Exact Numerical Determinism)**: Re-running identical shock on identical graph returns bit-exact identical floating-point floats.

#### 4. Copula & Tail Risk Test Suite ($R\text{-}01$ to $R\text{-}08$)
- Created [`backend/tests/unit/test_copula_cases.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/contagion-graph/backend/tests/unit/test_copula_cases.py):
  - **R-01 (Covariance Positive Semi-Definiteness)**: Correlation matrices pass Cholesky decomposition or spectral clipping.
  - **R-02 (Copula Uniform Marginals)**: Probability Integral Transform produces uniform $U(0, 1)$ marginal distributions.
  - **R-03 (Student-t Tail Dependence)**: Heavy-tailed Student-$t$ ($\nu=5$) generates higher joint crash probability than Gaussian copula.
  - **R-04 (Monotonicity of VaR Across Confidence Levels)**: $\text{VaR}_{99\%} > \text{VaR}_{95\%}$.
  - **R-05 (Expected Shortfall Subadditivity / Coherence)**: $\text{CVaR}_{99\%} \ge \text{VaR}_{99\%}$ holds for all asset distributions.
  - **R-06 (Marginal Contribution to Risk Additivity)**: $\sum_i \text{MCR}_i = \text{Total Portfolio VaR}$.
  - **R-07 (Kupiec POF Rejection)**: Backtest fails model if exceptions exceed binomial rejection threshold.
  - **R-08 (Zero Variance / Single Asset Stability)**: Degenerate covariance matrices handle single-asset cases gracefully without crashing.
