## 2. Mathematical Formulations & Algorithms Added

### 2.1 Almgren-Chriss (2000) Optimal Liquidation
Given position liquidation notional $V_i$, 30-day average daily volume $\text{ADV}_i$, annualized volatility $\sigma_i$, and market participation rate $\rho$:
- **Temporary Impact Slippage**:
  $$I_{\text{temp}, i} = \eta \cdot \sigma_i \cdot \sqrt{\frac{V_i}{\text{ADV}_i \cdot \rho}} \quad (\eta = 0.25)$$
- **Permanent Adverse Price Discovery**:
  $$I_{\text{perm}, i} = \gamma \cdot \frac{V_i}{\text{ADV}_i} \quad (\gamma = 0.15)$$
- **Stressed Bid-Ask Spread Blowout**:
  $$S_{\text{stressed}, i} = S_{\text{baseline}, i} \cdot \left(1.0 + 3.0 \cdot \frac{V_i}{\text{ADV}_i}\right)$$
- **Endogenous Fire-Sale Multiplier**:
  $$\xi = 1.0 + 0.40 \cdot \sum_{i=1}^N \left(\frac{V_i}{\text{ADV}_i}\right)^{1.5}$$
- **ISDA SIMM Margin Call**:
  $$M_{\text{call}} = 0.12 \cdot \text{DerivNotional} \cdot \left|\frac{\Delta S}{S_0}\right| \cdot \sqrt{\frac{10}{10}}$$

### 2.2 What-If Network Mutation & Resilience ROI
For topological graph mutation set $\mathcal{M} = \{m_1, \dots, m_k\}$ applied to adjacency matrix $W \mapsto W'$:
$$\text{Avoided Loss } \Delta \text{PnL} = \text{Loss}_{\text{mutated}}(W') - \text{Loss}_{\text{baseline}}(W) > 0$$
$$\text{Resilience ROI} = \frac{\Delta \text{PnL}}{\sum_{m \in \mathcal{M}} \text{CapEx}_m}$$
$$\text{Regulatory Capital Relief} = \text{CVaR}_{0.99}(W) - \text{CVaR}_{0.99}(W')$$
