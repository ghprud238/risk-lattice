## 2. Mathematical Formulations

### 2.1 Non-Linear Maritime Congestion Index ($C_i$)
$$C_i = \left(\frac{V_i}{V_{0, i}}\right) \cdot \left(\max\left(1.0, \frac{s_{0, i}}{\max(0.5, s_i)}\right)\right)^{0.8}$$
When military drills or canal obstructions force ships to halt or reroute (speed dropping from 16 to 3 knots), $C_i$ surges non-linearly, modeling vessel queue accumulation.

### 2.2 Demurrage Charter Rate Spike
$$\text{DemurrageSpike}_i = \max\left(0.0, \, (C_i - 1.0) \cdot 45.0\%\right)$$

### 2.3 Systemic Vulnerability Index (SVI)
$$\text{SVI} = 100 \cdot \sum_{i=1}^5 w_i \cdot \min\left(1.0, \, \frac{C_i - 1.0}{2.5} \cdot 0.6 + 0.4 \cdot \text{StatusPenalty}_i\right)$$
where $\text{StatusPenalty}_i \in \{0.15\text{ (Elevated)}, 0.50\text{ (Drill/Obstruction)}, 0.85\text{ (Blockade)}\}$.

### 2.4 Threat-to-Impulse Decaying Vector Mapping
$$s^{(0)}_j = \text{clamp}\left( -\left|\text{RawShock}\right| \cdot \mu_{\text{severity}} \cdot 0.85^j, \, -0.95, \, -0.02 \right)$$
where primary foundries ($j=0$) absorb the full direct impact, while subsidiary assemblers and module makers decay geometrically.
